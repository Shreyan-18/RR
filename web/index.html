Methodology & Mathematical Engine

Complete transparent formulation of how 200,999 deliveries were evaluated to isolate true clutch execution from reputation.

The Core Analytics Philosophy

Traditional cricket statistics suffer from severe context-blindness: a boundary hit when chasing 15 runs in 4 balls carries infinitely higher consequence than a boundary hit in the 3rd over of a dead rubber. Our methodology reconstructs the exact leverage of every delivery, separates Macro Stakes from Micro In-Game Situations, and benchmarks high-pressure performance ($S_{\text{hp}}$) against normal baseline performance ($S_{\text{norm}}$) to classify players across a 4-Quadrant archetype framework.

1

The Composite Pressure Index (P_ball)

Every legal delivery is assigned an instantaneous pressure value $P_{\text{ball}} \in [0, 1]$ uniting two orthogonal forces:

P_ball = 0.40 × C_macro + 0.60 × S_micro
  • C_macro: Pre-ball tournament context, stakes, and hostile fortresses.
  • S_micro: Live match state (dynamic RRR, balls remaining, wickets down).
  • Super Overs: Hardcoded to maximum pressure ($P = 1.00$).
  • 70th Percentile Split: Deliveries with $P \ge 0.70$ form the High-Pressure Regime (60,314 balls).
2

Macro Stakes Taxonomy (W_M)

Calculated before the delivery is bowled to reflect the severity of match consequences:

C_macro = 0.50 × W_M + 0.35 × Q_opp + 0.15 × H_fortress
  • Championship Finals ($W_M = 1.00$): 15 winner-take-all tournament finals.
  • Direct Knockouts ($W_M = 0.95$): 9 eliminators and semi-finals.
  • Tied Series Deciders ($W_M = 0.85$): 34 tied 1–1 or 2–2 bilateral deciders.
  • Marquee Rivalries ($+0.15$ boost): IND vs PAK, MI vs CSK, AUS vs ENG.
  • Dead Rubbers ($W_M = 0.35$): 64 matches after series was clinched.
3

Opposition Quality Hierarchy (Q_opp)

Calibrated on empirical win-loss records and ICC silverware between 2022 and 2026:

Tier 1.0 (1.00) India (82 wins, 2 T20 WCs)
Tier 1.5 (0.88-0.92) IPL Franchises
Tier 2.0 (0.85) Australia, England, SA, NZ

4

Psychological Momentum Indicators

  • Post-Boundary Stress (Composure): Evaluates what a bowler does on the ball immediately following a 4 or 6. Bumrah (46.8%) and Narine (40.0%) lead the tournament in comeback dot/wicket rate.
  • Post-Wicket Surge: Incoming batters face a $+0.08$ pressure surge for the next 6 balls as fielding momentum rises.
  • 3-Match Rolling Form: Accounts for fatigue and confidence shifts entering the fixture.
5

Performance Scoring Equations & Weights

Batting Performance Equation

Score = 0.30·SR + 0.25·Bdry% + 0.18·Rot% + 0.17·Avg + 0.10·DotAvoid

Prioritizes Boundary Conversion (25%) over strike rotation (18%) per franchise rules, since high-pressure death overs demand maximum boundaries.

Bowling Performance Equation

Score = 0.30·Econ + 0.25·WSR + 0.20·PostBdry + 0.15·Dot% + 0.10·Q_opp

Rewards low economy (30%) and wicket-taking strike rate (25%) alongside post-boundary bounce-back composure (20%).

Pressure Performance Ratio (PPR)

Measures how much a player elevates compared to normal conditions: $\text{PPR} = S_{\text{hp}} / \max(20.0, S_{\text{norm}})$. A ratio of $1.51$ (e.g. Naman Dhir) represents a $+51\%$ performance lift under pressure, while a ratio of $0.79$ (e.g. Sam Curran as a bowler) represents a $-21\%$ decline under pressure.
