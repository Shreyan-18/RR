Mathematical Formulation & Domain Architecture

Detailed breakdown of all mathematical equations, macro stakes, opposition weighting, and the composite pressure index.

1. The Composite Pressure Index

Every ball is assigned a continuous score $P_{\text{ball}} \in [0, 1]$ uniting consequence severity and micro leverage:

P_ball = 0.40 * C_macro + 0.60 * S_micro

Deliveries in the top 30% ($P \ge 0.70$) form the High-Pressure (HP) Regime, benchmarked directly against the normal regime. Super Overs are hardcoded to $1.00$.

2. Macro Stakes & Match Context

C_macro = 0.50 * W_M + 0.35 * Q_opp + 0.15 * H_fortress
  • Tournament Finals ($1.00$): 15 championship matches.
  • Direct Knockouts ($0.95$): 9 eliminator & semi-final fixtures.
  • Tied Series Deciders ($0.85$): 34 bilateral deciders & 5 Super 4s.
  • Dead Rubbers ($0.35$): 64 matches after series was clinched.
